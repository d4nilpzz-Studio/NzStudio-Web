title: NzKoth
---

NzKoth adds the best **King of the Hill** events to your server.

Available for `1.21^` builtin version `26.2`

## Features

 - Easy to use: select a zone with the wand and create a koth with one command
 - 2 game modes: `CAPTURE` and `SCORE`
 - In-game GUI editor for every koth setting and its rewards
 - In-game GUI scheduler: start or stop koths daily, every X hours or every X minutes, on chosen days
 - Rewards: console commands and items with chance and random amounts
 - Winner crown: a floating, spinning crown over the winner (custom model with the resource pack, glow, outline and color)
 - Holograms above the koth, with an idle view showing the next event
 - Builtin scoreboard and boss bar `[optional]`
 - Broadcasts for start, stop, capture, contested and win `[optional]`
 - Team mode: show the team of the capturer and track team wins (vanilla teams or any PlaceholderAPI placeholder)
 - Leaderboards: all-time, hourly, daily, weekly, monthly or custom periods (`12h`, `3d`, `2w`...) for players and teams
 - Starter items: give an item that starts a koth when right-clicked
 - Discord Webhooks on koth `start`, `stop`, `capture start`, `capture stop`, `capture` and `end`
 - Storage in `YAML`, `MySQL`/`MariaDB` or `PostgreSQL` (share leaderboards between servers)
 - PlaceholderAPI `[support]`
 - MiniMessage Text `[support]`
 - Developer API with Bukkit events
